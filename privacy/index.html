Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This policy explains how ScienceGloon ("we", "us") handles information when the ScienceGloon Publisher app accesses YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook on our behalf. ScienceGloon is operated from Spain. Contact: daniel@lagowski.es.

Who uses the app

The app is an internal tool. It is used only by the ScienceGloon team to publish our own videos to our own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and it has no third-party users.

Data we access

When a team member signs in and grants permission, the app may access, for our own accounts only:

  • YouTube (scopes youtube.upload and youtube): permission to upload videos and their covers to our own channel, to add them to our own playlists, and to read our own channel's information and video statistics.
  • TikTok (scopes user.info.basic, user.info.stats and video.upload): permission to read our own account's basic profile and follower count and to send videos we made to our own TikTok inbox as drafts. We review and publish every draft ourselves in the TikTok app.
  • Instagram and Facebook (permissions instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts, and, when granted, instagram_manage_insights, read_insights and business_management): permission to publish our own videos to our own Instagram account and Facebook Page and to read their basic information, engagement and view counts.

How we use it

We use this access only to publish ScienceGloon videos on our own accounts and to measure how our own videos perform. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties except as needed to deliver it to the platforms themselves.

Google API Services User Data Policy

ScienceGloon's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses the YouTube API Services; by using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

YouTube API Services

You can revoke the app's access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

TikTok and Meta

The app's use of TikTok follows the TikTok Terms of Service and the TikTok Developer Terms; its use of Instagram and Facebook follows the Meta Platform Terms.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored privately on our own machine, readable only by the app's operator, and never shared, sold or published. Video statistics are kept in our own database.

Retention and deletion

We keep tokens only while the app is in use and delete them, with the data obtained through them, within 30 days of revoking access or on request to daniel@lagowski.es.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask us for access to, correction or deletion of any personal data we hold, and you may complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page with a new effective date.
