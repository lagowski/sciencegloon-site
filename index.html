Ciencia explicada en un minuto.

ScienceGloon makes one short Spanish-language video a day that explains a science question (physics, maths, astronomy, how things work), simply and with its sources. Every number on screen comes with where it is from.

What the ScienceGloon app does

The ScienceGloon Publisher app is an internal publishing tool used only by the ScienceGloon team. It lets us:

  • upload our own finished videos, with their titles, descriptions and covers, to our own YouTube channel, TikTok account, Instagram account and Facebook Page;
  • read our own accounts' basic information and the statistics of our own videos, to check that uploads worked and to learn which videos people find useful.

The app does not offer accounts to the public and does not access anyone else's data.
